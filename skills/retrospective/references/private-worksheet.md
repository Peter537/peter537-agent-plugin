Claim states: **supported** means direct evidence establishes the stated scope; **bounded** limits the claim to what was demonstrated; **unresolved** leaves the unknown out of the conclusion; **unsupported** marks insufficient or contradictory support. Do not record an unsupported hypothesis as a decided fact.

For a future evaluation, preserve the destination's applicable activation/ownership, outcome, claim support, authorization, scope, and state-integrity questions. A valid no-change conclusion can complete the retrospective even when the alleged failure remains unresolved. Neither completion nor a chosen disposition establishes human approval or implementation.

Keep private values, source quotations, transcripts, conversation identifiers, credentials, canaries, sensitive hashes, and identifying paths out of every field. Do not print an unsanitized source to prepare this worksheet. Delete the task-created worksheet after reporting the bounded recommendation and required human-decision status.
