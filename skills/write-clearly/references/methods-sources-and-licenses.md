## Explicit non-goals

- Detecting or certifying whether a person or model wrote text.
- Evading AI-authorship detectors or optimizing a detector score.
- Using a fixed vocabulary blacklist as evidence of authorship or quality.
- Adding mistakes, randomness, anecdotes, or stereotypes to simulate a person.
- Applying one punctuation, rhythm, or tone rule to every language and genre.
- Certifying accessibility, translation, legal compliance, or controlled-language conformance.

## Maintenance

When updating the methodology, revisit mutable links, record the new access date, confirm licensing or attribution requirements, and preserve original wording. Add a source only when it materially changes runtime decisions; do not turn this reference into a catalog of writing advice.
