# Optional checkpoint outline

Adapt these headings to the task and retain an existing artifact's format. Omit irrelevant sections; keep consequential unknowns explicit. This outline never supplies authority or requires a persistent file.

- **Objective and acceptance:** the intended outcome and remaining acceptance claims.
- **Authority and scope:** what the user authorized, restrictions, and decisions with their source. The receiving agent must reconcile these historical notes with current authority.
- **Current state:** relevant repository and revision; staged, unstaged, untracked, or ignored work that matters; no sensitive machine details or values.
- **Completed work and evidence:** what actually happened, affected files, checks and their target state, results, and unverified claims. Retain useful failed approaches without raw logs.
- **Decisions and questions:** settled choices, assumptions, contradictions, and what would resolve uncertainty.
- **Remaining work:** the next bounded action, prerequisites, and useful local references.
- **Temporary state:** task-owned resources, purpose, current ownership evidence, retention needs, and incomplete cleanup. Revalidate before deleting or stopping anything.

For example, retain “reject negative quantities; zero remains valid” rather than shortening it to “validate quantities.” Record “not checked after the later edit” when applicable; do not turn a historical passing result into current proof.
