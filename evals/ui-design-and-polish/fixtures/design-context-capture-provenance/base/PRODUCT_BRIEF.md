# Dispatch comparison

Dispatch operators compare ordered route risks and assign one shipment without losing the evidence behind the choice. The default locale is British English; Danish is supported, but final Danish state labels remain unresolved.

The council owns design decisions and the authoritative context at `docs/product-design-context.md`. [ADR-031](decisions/ADR-031-comparison-variants.md) governs current desktop and tablet variants. Capture records document historical observations; their authors cannot approve new product decisions.
