# Dispatch console

Dispatch operators compare route risk and assign a shipment without leaving the queue. British English and Danish are supported locales. The web console prioritises dense comparison; the tablet variant prioritises larger touch targets. Permission-denied, stale-route, empty, loading, and assignment-error states are supported.
