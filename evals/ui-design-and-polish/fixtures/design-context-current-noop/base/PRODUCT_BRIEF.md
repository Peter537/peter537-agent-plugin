# Dispatch console

Dispatch operators compare ordered route risk and assign a shipment without leaving the queue. British English is the default locale, and Danish is also supported. The web console prioritises dense comparison; the tablet variant prioritises larger touch targets. Permission-denied, stale-route, empty, loading, and assignment-error states are supported.
